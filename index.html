How to use these at the table

Before the box comes out

  • Whoever is teaching skims How to play once. It is written to be read aloud — the bold lines are the script.
  • The Setup tab splits the job into lanes so five people can build the game at once instead of watching one person do it.
  • Tap the setup checkboxes as you go. They stay ticked on this device until you reset them.

Mid-game

  • FAQ is searchable. Type two or three words from the argument you're having and the answer opens itself.
  • Each game has a Quick ref tab: turn order and the four or five things everyone forgets, on one screen.
  • Tap the sun icon top-right to flip between the dark table view and a bright high-contrast one.

Put it on your Home Screen

  • Open this page in Safari on the iPad, tap the Share icon, then Add to Home Screen.
  • It launches full screen with no browser chrome, which is what you want on a crowded table.
