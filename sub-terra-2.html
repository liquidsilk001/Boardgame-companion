Sub Terra II: Inferno's Edge

A cooperative dash into a waking volcano. Lay tiles to map the temple, gather three keys, unlock the Sanctum and steal the Artifact — then outrun the eruption back to the entrance. Includes the Typhaon Wakes expansion.

  • 1–6 players
  • 60–90 min
  • Inside the Box Games (ITB)
  • Co-op · Typhaon Wakes 1-vs-many
  • Ages 10+

Fast setup

Sub Terra II has real setup — tiles, tokens, characters and a scenario to lay out — so this splits into lanes. The Typhaon Wakes content is folded in at the end; skip that block if you're playing the base game. Follow the scenario page in the rulebook for exact tile, guardian and key counts.

The one real win

Bag the components by system and keep Typhaon Wakes separate: base Temple tiles in one bag, the expansion's new tiles and guardians in another, and the Typhaon hazard deck in a third. Then adding or dropping the expansion is a one-bag decision, not a re-sort of the whole box.

Confirm against your scenario

Starting health per character, the number on the volcano track, and how many guardians and keys start in play all depend on the scenario and player count. Those exact numbers aren't reproduced here — read them off the scenario in your rulebook and treat that as final.

How to play — teach script

Teach with the Entrance tile down and figures on it. Bold lines read aloud; italics are notes to you. The one thing to land hard is what changes the moment someone lifts the Artifact.

0 · Buy-in (45 sec)

"This is a co-op treasure heist inside a waking volcano. We explore a temple by laying tiles, hunt down three keys, unlock the inner Sanctum, steal the Artifact — and then sprint back out the way we came before the whole place erupts. We win together; there's no traitor and no scoring against each other."

Frame it as Indiana Jones. The tension is the clock, not the other players.

1 · How you win — and lose

"We win the moment the explorer carrying the Artifact escapes back through the Entrance. Every other explorer who also makes it out is a bonus, but only the carrier has to get home. We lose if the volcano fully erupts before we've claimed the Artifact — or if the whole party is wiped out."

Stress: downed or lost explorers don't cost the win, as long as the Artifact gets out.

2 · Your turn — two actions

"On your turn you take two actions. Actions include: reveal a new tile onto an open edge; explore, which reveals a tile and steps you onto it in one go; move; heal; pick up a key or item; attack a guardian; or dig through a collapsed tile. You can take a third action if you really need it — but each extra action costs you one health."

Two actions, then optionally buy more with health. That trade is the core of the push-your-luck.

3 · The hazard phase — every turn ends here

"After your two actions, roll your danger die. Whatever it shows happens right now — a spike trap, arrows that reach across tiles, a cave-in, lava spreading, or a stumble that just costs you health. This is why you plan your actions carefully: the volcano always gets the last word on your turn."

In the Typhaon Wakes antagonist mode, the Typhaon player resolves this phase from their hazard cards instead of the die.

4 · Guardians & the clock

"At the end of each round, the guardians wake and move toward the nearest explorer, and can hurt you if they reach you. Also at the end of each round, the volcano track ticks down by one. When that track hits zero, it flips to its eruption side — the countdown is over and the mountain is ready to blow."

5 · Getting the Artifact — teach this properly

"You need three keys. Bring them to the Sanctum — which is placed at the far end of the temple once every tile is down — insert them, and one explorer lifts the Artifact. The instant it's lifted, everything gets worse: from now on you roll TWO danger dice every turn instead of one, and the volcano track counts down twice as fast. Grabbing the treasure is what really starts the disaster."

This is the pivot of the game. Make sure nobody lifts the Artifact until the party is ready to run.

6 · Eruption & the escape

"Once the volcano track is at zero, the next time anyone rolls lava, the volcano erupts. If we don't have the Artifact yet, that's game over. If we do, we run: tiles start turning to lava, and every lava roll flips every tile touching lava — it cascades. Get the Artifact carrier back to the Entrance and out."

Remind them the Entrance is a big multi-space tile, so plan the final steps home.

7 · When an explorer goes down

"Drop to zero health and you're down, not dead. Once per turn a downed explorer can crawl one step toward the Entrance or toward the rest of the party, and teammates can help you. So losing your footing isn't the end of your game."

Typhaon Wakes — the new threats

Volatile guardians can explode for heavy damage. Ranged guardians attack from a distance. The eternal guardian never stops — it won't rest until every explorer has left the temple. In the one-vs-many mode, the Typhaon player builds a hand of hazard cards and chooses which of these threats and traps land at the end of each explorer's turn.

First-game recommendations

  • Explore, don't just reveal. Exploring (reveal + step on) is usually more efficient than revealing tiles you then have to walk onto.
  • Split the keys. Don't put all three keys on one explorer who can be cut off; spread the risk.
  • Do not lift the Artifact early. The moment it's up, the clock doubles and you roll two dice a turn — be positioned to run first.
  • Keep a health buffer. Extra actions and stumbles both cost health; don't run to zero right before the escape.
  • The rulebook won't answer everything. Agree a sensible ruling on edge cases and keep moving.

Defer these — do not front-load

  • Every character's special ability
  • Each hazard's exact resolution
  • Individual guardian behaviours
  • Typhaon's hand-building details
  • Lava cascade edge cases

Teach the turn and the Artifact pivot; explain each hazard, ability and guardian the first time it actually comes up on the table.

Quick reference

Tap as keys are picked up. Three keys unlock the Sanctum. Resets when you reload.

All three keys — you can unlock the Sanctum and lift the Artifact. Be ready to run first.

Flip this when someone lifts the Artifact.

Artifact lifted — roll two danger dice every turn, and the volcano track counts down twice as fast. Get the carrier to the Entrance.

The turn, in three lines

  1. Take two actions (reveal / explore / move / heal / pick up / attack / dig). Extra actions cost 1 health each.
  2. Hazard phase: roll the danger die (or Typhaon resolves a hazard) and apply it now.
  3. End of round: guardians advance to the nearest explorer; the volcano track ticks down one.

The handful that break games

  1. Lifting the Artifact doubles the danger: two dice per turn and the clock counts down twice as fast.
  2. The volcano erupts only once the track is at zero and someone rolls lava — then lava cascades through all adjacent tiles.
  3. No Artifact when it erupts = loss. With the Artifact, you can still run for the Entrance.
  4. Only the Artifact carrier must escape to win; other escapees are a bonus.
  5. Downed explorers aren't out — once per turn they crawl toward the Entrance or the party.
  6. The Sanctum enters farthest from the Entrance once all Temple tiles are placed; the Entrance is a large multi-space tile.
  7. Typhaon Wakes: the eternal guardian never stops until the temple is empty of explorers.
