Faraway

A traveler's journey across the shifting continent of Alula. Lay a line of Region cards out over eight rounds — then score them on the way back, right to left, where each card only sees what lies beyond it.

  • 2–6 players
  • 15–30 min
  • Catch Up Games
  • Designers: Goupy & Lebrat
  • Ages 10+

Fast setup

Faraway sets up in about a minute. There are only two decks and a three-card hand, so this is short. Step numbers follow the official rulebook.

The one real win

Keep the two decks separated in the box — the big square Region cards (numbered 1–68) and the smaller Sanctuary cards. They shuffle and deal separately every game, and a mixed pile is the only thing that slows setup down.

Part 1 — the one-time prep

Nothing to punch or bag. Just make sure the two card types are told apart: Region cards are larger and carry a big number in the top-left; Sanctuary cards are smaller.

Part 2 — setup lanes

Two jobs, easily split.

Lane 1Decks & the river

One player

Steps 1–2. Shuffle the Region deck and place it in the middle. Shuffle the Sanctuary deck and set it beside it. Reveal Region cards face-up in a row — one more than the number of players — to form the shared display (the "river").

Lane 2Starting hands

Everyone

Step 3. Deal 3 Region cards to each player as their starting hand. Keep them hidden.

How to play — teach script

Teach with one Region card in hand so you can point at its corners. The bold lines are for reading aloud; the italics are notes to you, the teacher. There is exactly one idea people trip on — flag it early and often.

Say this three times

You play your cards left to right, but you score them right to left. A card only ever sees the cards to its right. Nearly every first-game mistake comes from forgetting this.

0 · Buy-in (30 sec)

"You're a traveler crossing the strange continent of Alula. Over eight rounds you'll lay down a line of Region cards — your journey out. At the very end you'll score them on the way back, from your last card to your first. Most fame wins."

The "out and back" framing is what makes the reverse scoring click. Lead with it.

1 · How you win

"Most fame — the game's word for points — at the end. Fame comes from your Region cards and from the Sanctuary cards you collect along the way."

2 · Reading a card

"Top-left is the number: how many hours this Region takes to explore. Every card 1 to 68 is unique. The number also decides turn order — lower goes first. The ring around it, and the card's background, tell you day or night. In the middle are elements — animal, mineral, plant — and clue icons that look like little maps. The bottom is how the card scores."

Don't drill the scoring symbols now. Just: number = time = turn order, and the bottom is points.

3 · A round — everyone at once

"Each round, everyone secretly picks one card from their hand and reveals it at the same time, adding it to the right-hand end of their own line."

All players play simultaneously; there's no waiting for turns to come around.

4 · Finding a Sanctuary — teach this properly

"If the card you just played has a HIGHER number than the card you played right before it, you 'find a Sanctuary.' Draw one Sanctuary card — plus one extra for each clue-map icon showing anywhere in your line. Look at them, keep just ONE, and put the rest back on the bottom of the Sanctuary deck."

"Sanctuary cards stay face-up in front of you for the rest of the game, and they count when you score."

Two catches to stress: (1) only a higher number triggers it — icons alone never do; (2) the extra draws only widen your choice, you still keep exactly one.

5 · Refilling — order matters

"Now refill back to three cards. In order of the number everyone just played — lowest first — each player takes ONE card from the face-up river into their hand. The river is NOT topped up until everyone has taken one, so whoever played the highest number picks last, from what's left."

This is the only direct interaction: a low number this round buys you first pick of the river next.

6 · The final round

"On the eighth and last round, everyone plays their final card and still resolves any Sanctuary they find — but we do NOT reveal a new river, because nobody will be drawing again."

7 · Scoring — the return journey

"Turn all eight of your Region cards face-down, but leave your Sanctuary cards face-up. Now flip them back up one at a time, starting from your LAST card on the right and moving left. Score each card as you reveal it, counting only the cards already face-up — itself and everything to its right — plus all your Sanctuary cards. A card that needs three animals only scores if those animals are already revealed to its right."

"When all eight Region cards are scored, add the points from your Sanctuary cards. Highest total fame wins."

Reveal slowly, right to left, and total as you go — it stops people from counting icons that aren't eligible yet.

Not applicable here

No first round Sanctuary. On round one there's no previous card to beat, so nobody finds a Sanctuary. No hidden agendas either — everyone's line and Sanctuaries are open information; only your hand is secret.

First-game recommendations

  • Climbing numbers earn Sanctuaries; a big drop earns nothing. Sometimes a lower card is still worth playing for turn order — decide which you want.
  • Collect a clue-map icon early. Every one you have widens your Sanctuary choice for the rest of the game.
  • Plan the right side first. Since cards only see what's to their right, the requirements you'll satisfy live to the right of the card that needs them.
  • Say "forwards then backwards" every time you explain a card. It's the whole learning curve.

Defer these — do not front-load

  • Day/night set-scoring cards
  • Colour-matching Region cards
  • Which specific icons each card wants
  • The 5-card advanced draft variant

Each is one sentence when the card is on the table, and a wall of confusion if explained up front.

Final round — play your last card, resolve any Sanctuary, but do not reveal a new river. Then score right → left.

The round, in four lines

  1. Everyone secretly plays one card to the right of their line, revealing together.
  2. Played a higher number than your last card? Find a Sanctuary: draw 1 + 1 per clue icon, keep one.
  3. Refill in number order, lowest first, one card each from the river (no top-up until all have drawn).
  4. After 8 rounds: flip Regions face-down, keep Sanctuaries up, and score right → left.

The handful that break games

  1. Play left → right, score right → left. The single most-missed rule.
  2. A card only counts cards already revealed to its right, plus your Sanctuaries. Nothing to its left exists yet.
  3. Only a higher number finds a Sanctuary. Icons alone never trigger one.
  4. Clue icons add extra draws, not extra keeps. You still keep exactly one Sanctuary.
  5. Refill order is lowest-number-first, and the river isn't topped up mid-refill.
  6. Round 1 gives no Sanctuary (no previous card); round 8 reveals no new river.
  7. Sanctuary cards stay face-up and score for everything.
